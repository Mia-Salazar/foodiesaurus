# Lectura fácil (español)

Follow Plena inclusión / IFLA easy-to-read practice. A good INFLESZ score is not enough if the text is still abstract, ironic, or dense.

## Language

- One idea per sentence. One topic per paragraph.
- Short sentences. Prefer subject + verb + object.
- Common words. If a needed term is hard (`alérgeno`, `provincia`), explain it the first time in plain words.
- Active voice. Address the reader as `tú`.
- Concrete facts. Avoid metaphors, double meaning, and irony (Rita’s joke on the home hero may stay; do not add more jokes in instructions, errors, or empty states).
- No double negatives. No stacked subordinate clauses.
- Avoid `/`, `&`, and unexplained abbreviations.
- Numbers as digits (`10`, not `diez`) when they are data.
- Lists instead of long comma chains.

## UI copy in this app

- Buttons say the action: `Buscar`, `Crear restaurante`, `Ver restaurante`.
- Errors say what happened and what to do next.
- Empty states say there is nothing, then offer one next step.
- Labels stay visible (`Nombre`, `Provincia`, `Ordenar`). Do not rely on placeholder only.
- Link text makes sense alone (`Volver al inicio`, not `aquí` or `click`).

## Layout

- Clear heading hierarchy (`h1` once per page, then `h2`…).
- Space between blocks. Do not pack walls of text.
- Images support the meaning; informative images have a short `alt`.
- Keep line length readable. Do not fully justify.
- Front-load the important words.

## Examples

```text
❌ No se pudieron cargar los restaurantes
✅ No hemos podido mostrar los restaurantes. Prueba otra vez.

❌ Elige una provincia para ver restaurantes.
✅ Elige una provincia. Así verás los restaurantes de esa zona.

❌ Resultados para “Friki”
✅ Restaurantes que coinciden con Friki
```
